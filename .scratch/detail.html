Soviet collider punches a hole through the fabric of reality in 1970. Three hours later, you are on your way to becoming the first ever human to step into a new dimension. What could go wrong? Well, a lot of things.

The Additional Dimension

You play as an Associate Professor sent on a routine, "honorary" mission: step into the newly discovered spatial anomaly, deploy a sensor array, and return. But the moment the device locks into place the radio cuts out, the elevator is gone.


You are trapped in a dimension outside of our realm that shouldn't exist. Now, your only task is to unravel the logic of this broken place and somehow get the hell out of the situation your supervisor Petrov put you in.


Key Features

  • Liminal-ish Space: What will you do if the hallway loops into itself?
  • Atmospheric Story-Driven Horror: A dense, slow-burn psychological nightmare inspired by classic sci-fi and analog dread.
  • Two Distinct Endings: Will you manage to correct the mathematical errors of the past, or will you be permanently integrated into the infrastructure of the base?
  • Estimated playtime for full playthrough is 30-40 minutes, though it depends.
